## 3. AI-specific acceptable use

The Service uses AI models that can produce inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or otherwise inappropriate output. When using AI features you must:

- **Review AI output before relying on it.** You are solely responsible for reviewing, validating, and verifying any AI-generated output before acting on it or making it available to others. AI output must not be treated as a substitute for human judgement.
- **Keep a human in the loop for significant decisions.** You must not use the Service to make fully automated decisions that produce legal effects, or similarly significant effects, on individuals (for example concerning employment, credit, housing, insurance, healthcare, or access to essential services) without meaningful human oversight and review.
- **Not engage in practices prohibited or restricted under the EU AI Act.** You must not use the Service to generate disinformation, to impersonate individuals, for social scoring, for biometric categorisation, or for any other practice that is prohibited or restricted under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the **EU AI Act**) or other applicable AI regulation. You are responsible for determining and complying with any obligations that apply to your particular use case.
- **Not present AI output as professional advice.** You must not present AI-generated output as professional legal, medical, financial, or other regulated advice, and you must not use it in a manner that requires a licensed professional unless an appropriately qualified person reviews and takes responsibility for it.
- **Comply with the AI transparency duty.** Where you deploy AI agents, chatbots, or other AI-generated interactions that engage with end-users, you must clearly inform those end-users that they are interacting with an AI system, and you must label AI-generated or AI-manipulated content where required by law.
