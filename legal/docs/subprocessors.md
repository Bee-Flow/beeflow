Bee Flow B.V. ("**Bee Flow**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") uses a limited number of carefully selected third parties to help us deliver the Bee Flow platform and related applications, APIs and services (together, the "**Service**"). This page lists the sub-processors that may process personal data on our behalf when we act as a processor for our Customers, and supports the transparency obligations in our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

A **sub-processor** is a third party engaged by Bee Flow to process personal data on our behalf in order to provide the Service — for example our cloud-hosting provider, our payment processor, or the AI model providers that power our AI-agent features. Each sub-processor is bound by a written data processing agreement that requires GDPR-compliant handling and imposes obligations no less protective than those in our own agreement with the Customer.

## Keeping this list current

We maintain this list so that it reflects the sub-processors we currently use. We will keep it up to date as our sub-processors change.

## Advance notice and right to object

Before we engage a new sub-processor, or replace an existing one, in a way that affects the processing of personal data, we will give affected Customers **at least 30 days' advance notice** (for example by email or through a notice in the Service, or by updating this page where Customers have subscribed to change notifications).

During that notice period a Customer may **object** to the new or replacement sub-processor on reasonable data-protection grounds. If a Customer raises such an objection, we will work together in good faith to address it — for example by proposing a commercially reasonable alternative or change in configuration. If we are unable to resolve the objection within a reasonable time, the Customer may **terminate the affected part of the Service** by written notice, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement.

## How AI model providers process your content

AI model providers receive prompts and content you submit to AI agents **only for the duration of the request** needed to generate a response. Where a provider offers a **no-training / zero-retention** setting, we enable it, so that your content is not used to train or improve the provider's models and is not retained beyond the request. Which provider is used depends on the **model the Customer selects** in the Service.

## EEA-resident sub-processors and international transfers

Sub-processors established in the European Economic Area require no additional transfer safeguards beyond the GDPR. Our **primary infrastructure (Scaleway)** keeps personal data in **EU data centres by default**, so data is processed and stored within the EEA unless a Customer chooses a feature or model that involves a provider located outside the EEA.

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA — in particular to the United States — we rely on the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** (for providers that are self-certified) and/or the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses** together with supplementary technical and organisational measures. Further detail on these safeguards is set out in Section 6 of our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
