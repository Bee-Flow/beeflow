## 1. Definitions

- **Applicable Data Protection Law** means all laws and regulations applicable to the processing of personal data under this DPA, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "**GDPR**"), the Dutch implementation thereof (the Uitvoeringswet AVG, "**UAVG**"), and, where applicable, any successor or equivalent legislation, together with binding guidance and decisions of competent supervisory authorities.
- **Controller** means the natural or legal person which, alone or jointly with others, determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data. For the purposes of this DPA, the Customer is the Controller.
- **Processor** means the natural or legal person which processes personal data on behalf of the Controller. For the purposes of this DPA, Bee Flow is the Processor.
- **Personal Data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined in Article 4(1) GDPR, that is contained within the Customer Content and processed by Bee Flow on the Customer's behalf under this DPA.
- **Processing** means any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, as defined in Article 4(2) GDPR (and "**process**", "**processes**" and "**processed**" are construed accordingly).
- **Customer Content** means any data, prompts, files, documents, conversations, agent configurations, knowledge-base material or other content that the Customer or an Authorised User submits to or generates within the Service, to the extent it contains Personal Data processed by Bee Flow on the Customer's behalf.
- **Sub-processor** means any third party engaged by Bee Flow (or by another Sub-processor) to process Personal Data on the Customer's behalf in connection with the Service.
- **Data Subject** means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom the Personal Data relates.
- **Authorised User** has the meaning given in the Terms.
- **Subscription Term** has the meaning given in the Terms.
- **EU SCCs** means the Standard Contractual Clauses set out in the Annex to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.
- **DPF** means the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (C(2023) 4745).
- **Personal Data Breach** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data, as defined in Article 4(12) GDPR.

## 2. Roles and scope

The parties acknowledge that, with respect to the processing of Customer Content, the Customer is the **Controller** and Bee Flow is the **Processor**. Where the Customer itself acts as a processor on behalf of a third-party controller, Bee Flow acts as a sub-processor; in that case the Customer warrants that it has the necessary authority and instructions from the relevant controller, and the obligations in this DPA apply to Bee Flow accordingly.

Bee Flow separately acts as an independent controller in respect of certain account, billing, security and diagnostic data it processes for its own purposes (such as account administration, fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations). Such processing is governed by the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and is outside the scope of this DPA.

The subject-matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of Personal Data and the categories of Data Subjects are set out in **Annex I**.

## 3. Processing on documented instructions

3.1 Bee Flow processes Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a third country or an international organisation, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which Bee Flow is subject. In such a case, Bee Flow will inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

3.2 The Terms, this DPA (including its Annexes), the configuration and settings the Customer selects within the Service, and the Customer's use of the Service in accordance with its documentation constitute the Customer's complete and final documented instructions for the processing of Personal Data. Additional or alternative instructions must be agreed in writing and may be subject to a change in fees where they require material additional effort.

3.3 Bee Flow will promptly inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR, the UAVG or other Applicable Data Protection Law. Bee Flow is not obliged to carry out a legal review of the lawfulness of the Customer's instructions, and this Section 3.3 is without prejudice to the Customer's responsibility as Controller for the lawfulness of the processing it instructs.

## 4. Confidentiality

Bee Flow ensures that persons authorised to process the Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality. Bee Flow limits access to Personal Data to those of its personnel who need access to perform Bee Flow's obligations under the Terms, and ensures that such personnel are subject to confidentiality undertakings and appropriate training in respect of their data-protection responsibilities.

## 5. Security of processing

5.1 Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons, Bee Flow implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR.

5.2 The technical and organisational measures in force at the date of this DPA are described in **Annex II**. Bee Flow may update these measures from time to time provided that the updated measures do not materially reduce the overall level of security of the Personal Data.

5.3 In assessing the appropriate level of security, Bee Flow takes account in particular of the risks presented by processing, in particular from accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Personal Data transmitted, stored or otherwise processed.

## 7. Assistance to the Controller

7.1 **Data-subject rights.** Taking into account the nature of the processing, Bee Flow assists the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in fulfilling the Customer's obligation to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Articles 12 to 23 GDPR (including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection). Where Bee Flow receives a request directly from a Data Subject relating to Customer Content, it will not respond to that request itself (except to acknowledge receipt and direct the Data Subject to the Customer where appropriate) but will, without undue delay, forward the request to the Customer.

7.2 **Security, breach, DPIA and prior consultation.** Taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Bee Flow, Bee Flow assists the Customer in ensuring compliance with the obligations pursuant to Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, namely: security of processing (Article 32); notification of a Personal Data Breach to the supervisory authority (Article 33) and communication to Data Subjects (Article 34); data protection impact assessments (Article 35); and prior consultation with the supervisory authority (Article 36).

7.3 Bee Flow may charge a reasonable fee for assistance under this Section 7 to the extent the assistance required exceeds the functionality made available within the Service, save where the assistance is required as a result of Bee Flow's breach of this DPA.

## 8. Personal Data Breach notification

8.1 Bee Flow notifies the Customer **without undue delay** after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Content, and in any event in time to allow the Customer to meet its own notification deadlines under Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

8.2 The notification will, to the extent known and as information becomes available, include: (a) a description of the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including where possible the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and Personal Data records concerned; (b) the name and contact details of the point of contact where more information can be obtained; (c) a description of the likely consequences of the breach; and (d) a description of the measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.

8.3 Bee Flow's notification of, or response to, a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgement by Bee Flow of any fault or liability.

## 9. Deletion or return of Personal Data

9.1 At the Customer's choice, Bee Flow deletes or returns all Personal Data to the Customer after the end of the provision of services relating to processing, and deletes existing copies, unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the Personal Data.

9.2 The Customer may export Customer Content through the functionality of the Service during the Subscription Term and for a grace period of up to **30 days** after termination or expiry. After that grace period, Bee Flow deletes or anonymises the Personal Data within its production systems, and removes it from routine backups in accordance with its backup-rotation cycle (typically within a further 90 days), save for any Personal Data that Bee Flow is required to retain by law (for example, billing records retained for the period required by Dutch tax and accounting law). Personal Data retained under a legal-retention obligation remains protected by the security measures in **Annex II** and is processed only for the purpose of the applicable legal obligation.

9.3 On the Customer's written request, Bee Flow provides written confirmation that it has complied with this Section 9.

## 10. Audits and inspections

10.1 Bee Flow makes available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in Article 28 GDPR and this DPA, and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or another auditor mandated by the Customer.

10.2 The Customer may exercise its audit right by requesting Bee Flow's then-current security documentation, policies, and any third-party audit reports or certifications that Bee Flow holds. 10.3 Where the documentation under Section 10.2 is not sufficient to demonstrate compliance, the Customer may conduct an on-site or remote inspection, subject to the following: (a) the Customer gives at least 30 days' prior written notice; (b) inspections take place during normal business hours, no more than once per twelve-month period (save where required by a supervisory authority or following a Personal Data Breach affecting the Customer); (c) the Customer and its auditors are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations and do not access the data of any other Bee Flow customer; (d) the inspection does not unreasonably disrupt Bee Flow's business; and (e) the Customer bears its own and Bee Flow's reasonable costs of the inspection, unless the inspection reveals a material breach by Bee Flow.

## 11. International transfers

11.1 Bee Flow processes and stores Customer Content within the European Economic Area (EEA) by default; the primary cloud infrastructure is hosted with Scaleway in EU data centres. Certain Sub-processors (in particular AI model providers and certain payment and identity providers) may process Personal Data outside the EEA, in particular in the United States, as described in **Annex III** and **Annex IV**.

11.2 Where Personal Data is transferred to a country outside the EEA that is not covered by an adequacy decision, the transfer is made subject to appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR, as set out in **Annex IV**, namely:

- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework.** Where a US-based Sub-processor is self-certified under the **DPF** for the relevant categories of data, the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023. The Sub-processor's current certification status can be verified on the [public DPF list](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list).
- **EU Standard Contractual Clauses.** Where a Sub-processor is **not** certified under the DPF, or as a complementary safeguard, the transfer relies on the **EU SCCs**, incorporated by reference as set out in Section 11.3, together with the supplementary technical and organisational measures described in **Annex II** and **Annex IV**.

11.3 The **EU SCCs** are incorporated into this DPA by reference and apply to any transfer to which they are stated to apply in **Annex IV**, as follows: (a) **Module Two (controller-to-processor)** applies where the Customer is a controller and the data importer is a processor; (b) **Module Three (processor-to-processor)** applies where the Customer acts as a processor and the data importer is a sub-processor; (c) Clause 7 (the **docking clause**) applies; (d) in Clause 9, **Option 2 (general written authorisation)** applies, with the notice period set out in Section 6.2; (e) in Clause 11, the optional independent-dispute-resolution language does not apply; (f) in Clause 17, the governing law is the law of the Netherlands; (g) in Clause 18, the competent courts are the courts of the Netherlands; and (h) the Annexes to the EU SCCs are populated by **Annex I**, **Annex II** and **Annex III** of this DPA. Where there is any conflict between the EU SCCs and this DPA, the EU SCCs prevail in respect of the transfer they govern.

11.4 Bee Flow conducts, and keeps under review, a transfer-impact assessment for transfers relying on the EU SCCs, taking into account the circumstances of the transfer, the laws and practices of the third country of destination, and the supplementary measures in place. A summary is available on request to tomkooy@beeflow.nl.

11.5 **Accuracy note on specific providers.** Transfers to **Anthropic PBC** rely on the **EU SCCs** and supplementary measures; Anthropic is **not** self-certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and Bee Flow does not represent otherwise. Where other US Sub-processors (such as Google LLC, OpenAI, Microsoft Corporation and Stripe, Inc.) are DPF-certified for the relevant data, transfers may rely on the DPF, with the EU SCCs available as a fallback safeguard.

## 12. AI processing commitments

12.1 Bee Flow does **not** use Customer Content to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop generalised or non-personalised AI models.

12.2 Where Customer Content is forwarded to AI model Sub-processors as part of providing the Service, it is forwarded only for the duration necessary to process the relevant request and to return a response. Where the AI model provider offers a no-training and/or zero-retention setting, Bee Flow enables it, so that the provider does not use Customer Content to train its models and does not retain it beyond the request (save for any limited abuse-monitoring retention that the provider applies and that cannot be disabled, which is described in the provider's terms).

12.3 The provider used for a given request depends on the model the Customer or Authorised User selects. The categories of AI model Sub-processors are listed in **Annex III**.

## Annex II — Technical and organisational measures (TOMs)

Bee Flow maintains the following technical and organisational measures pursuant to Article 32 GDPR. These measures may be updated as described in Section 5.2.

| Area | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Encryption in transit** | All connections to the Service use TLS (HTTPS). Traffic between internal services and to Sub-processors is encrypted in transit. |
| **Encryption at rest** | Personal Data is encrypted at rest where supported by the hosting and storage layer. |
| **Access control and least privilege** | Role-based access controls and the principle of least privilege govern staff and system access. Administrative access is restricted to authorised personnel, requires authentication, and is subject to confidentiality undertakings. |
| **Authentication** | Support for strong authentication, including single sign-on and multi-factor authentication where configured by the Customer. Account credentials are stored as salted hashes. |
| **Audit logging** | Security-relevant events and administrative actions are logged to support detection, investigation and accountability. |
| **Pseudonymisation / PII tokenisation** | A PII tokenisation capability (Privacy Shield) detects and replaces direct identifiers with tokens before content is forwarded to AI model Sub-processors, where enabled by the Customer, reducing the Personal Data exposed to third parties. |
| **Tenant isolation** | Logical separation of Customer data so that one customer cannot access another customer's data. |
| **Backup and recovery** | Regular backups with defined retention and rotation, supporting restoration in the event of loss or corruption. |
| **Vulnerability management** | Patching and dependency management, and review of the security posture, to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in a timely manner. |
| **Breach detection** | Monitoring and alerting to detect anomalous activity and potential Personal Data Breaches, supporting the notification obligations in Section 8. |
| **Network and infrastructure security** | Hosting with an EEA-based infrastructure provider (Scaleway) in EU data centres; network controls and isolation between environments. |
| **Personnel** | Confidentiality undertakings and data-protection awareness for personnel with access to Personal Data. |
| **Sub-processor assurance** | Written contracts imposing equivalent data-protection obligations on Sub-processors (Section 6). |
| **Data minimisation and deletion** | Deletion and return processes (Section 9) and retention limits to avoid keeping Personal Data longer than necessary. |
