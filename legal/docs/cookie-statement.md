## 1. What cookies and similar technologies are

A **cookie** is a small text file that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit it. Cookies allow the website to recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as whether you are signed in.

We also use **similar technologies**, in particular your browser's **localStorage** and **sessionStorage**. These store small amounts of data in your browser rather than sending it back to our servers with every request, but they serve comparable purposes (for example, remembering your interface preferences). In this statement, references to "cookies" include these similar storage technologies unless stated otherwise.

Cookies and storage entries can be:

- **Session** — temporary, and deleted when you close your browser; or
- **Persistent** — kept for a defined period, or until you delete them, so they are available on your next visit.

## 2. The legal framework

In the Netherlands, the use of cookies and similar technologies is governed by the **Telecommunicatiewet (Article 11.7a)**, which implements the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, read together with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Under these rules:

- **Strictly necessary cookies** — those that are technically required to deliver a service you have explicitly requested (such as keeping you signed in or protecting a form against cross-site request forgery) — do **not** require your prior consent.
- **Non-essential cookies** — such as analytics, marketing, advertising, or cross-site tracking cookies — **do** require your prior, informed, opt-in consent before they may be placed.

The equivalent rule in other EEA countries follows the same ePrivacy Directive. In **Germany**, for example, the corresponding provision is **§25 of the Telekommunikation-Digitale-Dienste-Datenschutz-Gesetz (TDDDG)** (formerly the TTDSG), which likewise exempts strictly necessary cookies from the consent requirement and requires consent for all others.

Because Bee Flow currently places only strictly necessary cookies and storage, no prior consent is required for the cookies described below.

You will be able to accept or reject these cookies, and we will not set them unless and until you have given consent.

Where you have given consent, you may **withdraw it at any time** — for example through the cookie banner or preferences control — and withdrawing consent will be as easy as giving it. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before you withdrew it.

## 5. How to control or delete cookies

You can control and delete cookies and stored data directly through your browser. Most browsers let you view the cookies and storage that have been set, delete them individually or in bulk, and block or restrict cookies from being set. You can usually find these options in your browser's privacy or security settings:

- **Google Chrome** — Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Mozilla Firefox** — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Apple Safari** — Settings → Privacy
- **Microsoft Edge** — Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that the cookies and storage described in this statement are **strictly necessary** for the Service to function. If you block or delete them — in particular the authentication, session, and CSRF cookies — you will **not be able to sign in or use the Service properly**.
